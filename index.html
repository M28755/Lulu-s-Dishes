Our Heritage

A Warm Family Table in the Heart of Mombasa

Lulu's Delish is built on a tradition of hospitality, coastal culture, and unforgettable flavors.

Located along bustling Narok Road in Majengo, we bring families, friends, and visitors together over fragrant pilau, slow-cooked biryani, and handmade chapatis. Every dish is crafted with fresh local ingredients, home-ground spices, and genuine warmth.
